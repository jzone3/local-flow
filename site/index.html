Local Flow

Hold a key. Talk. Let go. Your words appear wherever your cursor is — and nothing leaves your Mac.

Download for Mac Source on GitHub

macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon and Intel · free and open source

Listening

The bar while you speak. Liquid Glass on macOS 26.

Local by default

Whisper runs on your Mac's GPU. Pick Best, Classic or Tiny; Best is the default.

Or your own key

OpenAI, ElevenLabs or Gemini with retries and automatic fallback to the local model. Setup finds keys already in your shell.

One dictionary

Names, products, jargon. Used as a hint to the model and to fix spelling on-device. Pick word packs for your field during setup.

Cleanup without a cloud

Ums, uhs, capitalisation and punctuation are fixed with rules on your Mac. On by default, one switch to turn off.

Nothing kept

Transcripts and recordings stay on your Mac and are deleted when you quit, or after 12 hours. Always.

Open source

MIT licensed. Adding a provider is one Swift file. Updates install themselves from GitHub Releases.
