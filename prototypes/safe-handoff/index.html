What is real, and what is simulated?

Real in this prototypeEditable fields, destination and caret checks, holding a result, explicit insertion, discard, cancellation, and clipboard copy on request. No storage, analytics, microphone, or external requests. Copying intentionally moves text to your system clipboard; reload cannot clear that copy.

Simulated, not native verificationBoth apps, transcription, and the five-second delay. “Without guard” models the current insertion policy from source, not a recorded macOS reproduction. The prototype cannot inspect or paste into other apps; a native version would still need Accessibility identity and paste-race testing.
